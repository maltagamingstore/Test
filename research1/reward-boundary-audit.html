AUDIT ADDENDUM · 29 SEPTEMBER 2026

Reward calculation
numerical limitation.

A confirmed boundary artifact remains in the frozen model

In one authenticated recorded example, ordinary floating-point arithmetic produced tiny positive own and competing scores at the reward-band boundary. Dividing these scores produced a nonzero modeled reward share. Recalculating both scores from the same raw prices with exact decimal arithmetic gave zero. Reproducing the frozen calculation therefore does not establish numerical correctness.

The published estimates retain their original calculation. They describe partial, conditional gross model outputs. They are not earned rewards, payouts, spendable cash, or forecasts. Trading P/L and the handling rules are unchanged.

Research 4: the separate recorded replay retains the same known numerical limitation. Its diagnostic cohort contains 43 accepted near-zero-score intervals in 13 markets, contributing approximately $0.005059 in the main model and $0.001954 in the side-sum model. These are existing model contributions, not corrections or an exhaustive error bound. Open Research 4.

The three nested diagnostic thresholds—positive own plus competing score below 10−24, 10−18 and 10−12 weighted shares—selected the same candidates within each research. They are diagnostic thresholds, not new eligibility rules or independent repetitions. Market-hours sum simultaneously quoted markets. Each research retains its own entry window and excludes its exit-only follow-up from reward exposure.

Missing or conflicting reward inputs remain unknown. Aggregate order-book competition is an assumption about reward allocation, not an observation of our actual reward entitlement. A corrected numerical projection would require its own specified method and complete reconciliation; this addendum does not silently retune the original studies.

Evidence and review scope

The accepted legacy census covers 22,082,052 original intervals. Exact candidate membership was checked against each interval's recorded native settings source. A separate full-ledger reproduction, three defender reviews, five attacker reviews and a fresh synthesis support this qualified disclosure. Raw decimal failure receipts remain preserved. This does not certify complete source acquisition, unseen numerical errors or real exchange payments.
